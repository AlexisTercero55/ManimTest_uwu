CLI (high quality output and resolution output)

    Render as high quality
        > manim -pqh scene.py sceneClassName

        The -p flag plays the animation once it is rendered.

        As discussed previously, the -ql specifies low render 
        quality. This does not look very good, but is very useful 
        for rapid prototyping and testing. The other options that 
        specify render quality are -qm, -qh, and -qk for medium, 
        high, and 4k quality, respectively.

        Manim has created a new folder media/videos/1080p60, 
        which corresponds to the high resolution and the 60 
        frames per second.

    Change output resolution
        from CLI: manim render --help  we can change resolution as

            > manim -pqh -r "1080,1350" scene.py sceneClassName
